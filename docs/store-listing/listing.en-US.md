# Google Play listing (en-US)

## App name

Liddle Puzzles

## Short description

Eight offline puzzles, from Sudoku to Daily Word. No ads or accounts

## Full description

A few quiet minutes, a good puzzle, and that moment when the next move clicks.

Start with a Sudoku, see what you can make of today's five-letter word, or fill in a Nonogram one clue at a time. Liddle Puzzles brings eight games together, with no ads, no account, and no internet connection needed to play.

- Sudoku: Pick Easy, Medium, or Hard, and pencil in candidates as you go.
- Daily Word: Guess a new five-letter word each day using feedback on every guess.
- Nonograms: Turn the clues along each row and column into a picture.
- Takuzu: Balance zeroes and ones across the grid.
- Futoshiki: Fit the numbers into the grid so every inequality is true.
- Minesweeper: Use the numbers to find safe squares and flag mines.
- Numberlink: Join matching numbers without crossing paths.
- Slitherlink: Follow the clues to draw one continuous loop.

Play a little, then come back when you have time. Your progress stays on your device, so you can switch games without losing your place.

Want to compare solves? Share the exact starting puzzle in seven modes (all except Minesweeper), without sending your moves or notes. If a chat app doesn't open the link, paste it into Shared puzzles in Liddle Puzzles.
